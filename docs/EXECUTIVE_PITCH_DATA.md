## Capital Partner Readout

- The primary value proposition is trusted transaction infrastructure: verification reduces avoidable fraud exposure while escrow and evidence improve completion confidence.
- Revenue is diversified across subscriptions, transaction fees, escrow service fees, services, and attributed partner leads. Actual rates are plan- and campaign-controlled in basis points.
- Present target GMV, revenue, and valuation as scenario outputs with currency, period, assumptions, source, and update date visible.
- Do not describe a target as booked revenue, a valuation range as a commitment, or any scenario as a guaranteed investor return.
- Strategic pricing, geographic expansion, capital allocation, and partner terms require human approval and an auditable decision record.
