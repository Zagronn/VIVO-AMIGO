# VIVOAMIGO Cinematic Promo

**Title:** Guatemala Comercio Seguro y Garantizado  
**Slogan:** Llevamos Vidas  
**Runtime:** 45 seconds  
**Format:** 16:9 master with 9:16 and 1:1 cutdowns

## Creative Direction

Warm dawn light, premium graphite vehicles, restrained VIVO orange and cyan security accents, and confident human movement. The story moves from uncertainty to verified action: secure the funds, verify the vehicle, support the journey.

## Production Guardrails

- Use only fictional or consented app data; never show raw PAN, DPI, OTP, location history, or government response payloads.
- Do not depict a SAT/PNC result as a government endorsement. Use “verificación sujeta a disponibilidad y validación del proveedor” in campaign metadata where required.
- The escrow visual must show funds held, never imply automatic release from a click.
- Use licensed footage or original captures for Guatemala City, vehicles, uniforms, music, and voiceover.
- Produce captions in Spanish and Turkish for the slogan and keep critical information inside mobile-safe title margins.
